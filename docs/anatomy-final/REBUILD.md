# Male atlas rebuild

The new atlas prioritizes coverage, alignment and reviewable provenance over the previous 2 MB limit. Eleven gross-anatomy systems; organ internals and physiology lessons are deferred.

Z-Anatomy candidate revision: b9c9f98066e1e786814603b047c5bd3638c2a864. Its License.txt explicitly includes NC kidney and inner-ear contributions, plus insufficiently identified University of Washington brain/white-matter sources. These regions must be excluded from Z-derived exports unless individually cleared. CC-BY BodyParts3D replacements require measured registration against shared landmarks. Do not copy definitions, bundled scripts, unverified images, or textures. Blender is an offline GPL authoring tool, not shipped in the website.

Represent named structures with explicit primary and secondary systems, parent regions, source records and review status. Only one geometry instance per anatomical structure in the assembled scene. The study board repeats cross-listed structures only as labelled educational views, never duplicated anatomical tissue.

Board: labelled system groups, normalized study tiles, 10 CSS px padding each side (20 px between silhouettes), stable alphabetical order, orthographic camera, pan/zoom, non-anatomical-scale notice. Tile selection opens a rotatable inspection and restores the board when closed. Reduced motion switches immediately. Skin acts as an enclosing layer; system-only inspection reveals hidden anatomy.

Masters remain at source quality. Overview is bounded-error simplified; regional detail is fetched on selection. No invented vasculature, muscle insertions, or organ interiors. Procedural surface finish is visual styling, explicitly distinct from anatomical structure. Clinical/anatomical review remains pending until performed by a qualified reviewer.
